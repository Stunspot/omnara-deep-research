# Description

Research questions that exceed a quick search and summary are the territory of OMNARA. It decomposes the inquiry, maps the source terrain, tracks evidence and contradictions, closes decision-relevant gaps, and audits consequential citations.

The campaign remains resumable throughout, making OMNARA suited to investigations, literature reviews, market landscapes, policy research, and other work that must survive many sources and explicit uncertainty.

The campaign room keeps the recorded claims and cited sources in one navigable evidence field, so you can spot an unsupported assertion, follow a source across the argument, inspect the native note, compare two readings, find unresolved questions and read the synthesis with citations close at hand. Tracework, Survey Folio and Proof Cabinet are distinct working environments: a dark optical instrument, a paper field file and an oxblood evidence cabinet. All work on the same native files as your research agent; citation lines and source counts never certify support or completion.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from "Additional Files" to your Codex, Claude Code, or other harness, or add the .zip to a project knowledge base in Chat. Attach or reference the .zip in chat and say, "Install this Augment." The prompt in this post is optional.

- Extract the kit and run Open.cmd on Windows or Open.command on macOS with Python 3.10+.
- Select your existing campaign home with --data-root or OMNARA_HOME. The launcher starts or reconnects the matching local service, including after reboot.
- Create or import a native campaign, inspect claims and source notes, and read the self-contained report. Save before exporting native text or a next-pass handoff; media and external corpora retain their original custody.
- If evidence or scope changes, preserve the earlier answer and review the changed work. A recorded supported label cannot approve different source material.
- Shortcuts require consent. Native macOS execution remains unverified.

- Start with the decision or question, scope, time horizon, preferred sources, exclusions, and the kind of deliverable you need.
- Live web access is important for current or obscure topics. You can also attach papers, reports, notes, and existing research.
- Tell it when enough is enough: a deadline, source budget, or decision threshold keeps deep research from becoming bottomless research.
