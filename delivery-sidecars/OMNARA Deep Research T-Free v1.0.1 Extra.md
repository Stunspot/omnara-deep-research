# Description

Research questions that exceed a quick search and summary are the territory of OMNARA. It decomposes the inquiry, maps the source terrain, tracks evidence and contradictions, closes decision-relevant gaps, and audits consequential citations.

The campaign remains resumable throughout, making OMNARA suited to investigations, literature reviews, market landscapes, policy research, and other work that must survive many sources and explicit uncertainty.

# Usage Notes

Copy the `.zip` from Additional Files to the chosen harness or Chat project, attach or reference it in chat, and say, `Install this Augment.`

- Start with the decision or question, scope, time horizon, preferred sources, exclusions, and the kind of deliverable you need.
- Live web access is important for current or obscure topics. You can also attach papers, reports, notes, and existing research.
- Tell it when enough is enough: a deadline, source budget, or decision threshold keeps deep research from becoming bottomless research.

Public GitHub Repo: [omnara-deep-research](https://github.com/Stunspot/omnara-deep-research)
